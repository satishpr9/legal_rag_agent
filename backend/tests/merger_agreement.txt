Acme Corp & Beta Inc. Merger Agreement

Section 1.1: Parties. This Merger Agreement is entered into on July 19, 2026, by and between Acme Corporation, a Delaware corporation ("Acme"), and Beta Incorporated, a California corporation ("Beta").

Section 4.2: Purchase Price. The total purchase price for the acquisition of Beta by Acme shall be Fifty Million Dollars ($50,000,000) payable in cash at Closing.

Clause 8.3: Governing Law. This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action (whether in contract, tort or statute) that may be based upon, arise out of or relate to this Agreement, shall be governed by, and enforced in accordance with, the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

Clause 10.4: Termination. This Agreement may be terminated at any time prior to the Closing Date:
(a) by mutual written consent of Acme and Beta;
(b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred on or before December 31, 2026; or
(c) by Acme if there has been a material breach of representation or warranty by Beta.
